01 • Hook & Problem

The Empty Pharmacy Shelf

How does a clinic run out of life-saving medicine when the central ledger says stock is fine?

The Shelf Reality

Metformin 500mg: Stockout Today

A diabetic patient arrives at a Community Health Centre and is sent home empty-handed because zero usable pills exist on the shelf.

The Central Ledger Myth

• Ledger Balance: 450 units (Looks healthy)
• QA Quarantine Hold: -400 units (Unusable)

The Root Challenge: Passive health software tracks accounting balances, not physical reality. It ignores batch quarantines, expiration windows, delivery slip, and transit breakage until clinic shelves are already bare.

05 • Core Innovation

The Strict Zero-Hallucination Law

LLMs never perform arithmetic. Code computes numbers; models explain causes.

✕ The Fragile LLM Anti-Pattern

LLM prompts calculate stock cover and dosages: "Based on your stock, you have 4.2 days left." Leads to dosage drift, non-deterministic reorder quantities, and audit failures.

✓ The MedTrace Deterministic Standard

Zero LLM arithmetic. DeterministicEngine computes usable stock, shortfall, lead times, and costs. Adversarial Skeptic/Number Auditor rejects any LLM output quoting an unverified figure.

Deterministic Invariant:
Usable Stock = max(0, Ledger - Audit Drift - Quarantined - Expiring Batch Units)
Say: "Here is our most important technical rule: the LLM never does math. In medicine, an AI hallucinating days of cover is unacceptable. All arithmetic — usable stock, shortfall units, financial costs, Monte Carlo lead times — is pure deterministic code. The models only synthesize and explain."
06 • Reasoning Architecture

Adversarial Multi-Agent Loop

Tasks delegated by context; hypotheses challenged and revised across iterative debate rounds.

1. Reconciler

Audits physical counts, batches, and FDA recall signals into the Meterless World Model.

2. Investigator

Formulates 3 competing hypotheses (e.g. Phantom Drift vs Supplier Slip vs Market Shortage).

3. Skeptic Auditor

Challenges hypotheses, verifies arithmetic consistency, and demands SQL evidence.

4. Action Planner

Ranks peer transfers and PO expedites, factoring in transport capacity and H-MEM memory.

Iterative Self-Correction: If the Skeptic identifies arithmetic or evidentiary inconsistencies, the Investigator revises likelihoods across up to 3 rounds. If consensus fails, it safely escalates to human review with full provenance.
Say: "MedTrace is not a linear chain. It's an adversarial debate. The Investigator generates three competing hypotheses with SQL confirmation queries. The Skeptic rigorously challenges every assumption and audits every number. If an inconsistency is detected, the Investigator must revise."
